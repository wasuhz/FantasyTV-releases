# FantasyTV

Fantasy football AutoTune for Android TV with your own IPTV (Xtream or Dispatcharr).

## Install

On your Android TV / Google TV / Shield / Fire TV, open **Downloader** and enter:

https://github.com/wasuhz/FantasyTV-releases/releases/latest/download/FantasyTV.apk

Open FantasyTV, scan the QR code with your phone, and enter your Sleeper username and IPTV login.
Your logins stay on your TV.

## Updates

FantasyTV checks for new versions itself and shows **Update available** on the TV. Press OK.
