# FantasyTV

> **Public beta.** FantasyTV works, but it's new and tested on a handful of TVs and phones. Expect rough edges, and
> please report what breaks in [Issues](https://github.com/wasuhz/FantasyTV/issues). The app installs fixes itself.

NFL RedZone for your own fantasy teams, with your own IPTV (Xtream or Dispatcharr). One Android app for your **TV** and
your **phone**, foldables included. More at [fantasytvapp.com](https://fantasytvapp.com).

## Works on

Android 8.0 or newer, the same download for every device:

- **Android TV / Google TV:** NVIDIA Shield, onn 4K, Chromecast with Google TV, most Google TVs
- **Fire TV:** should work, untested
- **Android phones and tablets**; works well on the **Galaxy Fold** (folded, unfolded or half-open)
- Not iPhone, iPad or Apple TV (an iPhone can still be the remote for a TV, in its browser)

## Install

Downloader code **1790534** always gets the newest version, on a TV or a phone.

**On a TV:** open **Downloader** (by AFTVnews, from the TV's app store), enter **1790534** and install. Open FantasyTV
and scan its QR code with your phone to set it up.

**On a phone or Fold:** in the phone's browser, open **aftv.news/1790534** (or
[download the APK](https://github.com/wasuhz/FantasyTV-releases/releases/latest/download/FantasyTV.apk)), allow
installs from the browser when Android asks, and install. Open FantasyTV: it walks you through setup right on the
phone.

Either way: your stream server first, then your Sleeper or ESPN teams. Your logins stay on your device.

## Updates

FantasyTV checks for new versions itself. On a TV it shows **Update available**: press OK. Or turn on automatic updates
in Settings; they install when no game is live.
